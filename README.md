# CS 330 – Final Project Reflection

# How do I approach designing software?

I approach software design by breaking complex problems into simple components and building upward from there. In this project, I reduced a real-world desk setup into basic shapes and focused first on layout and spatial relationships before adding detail. This reinforced that strong design comes from clarity and structure rather than complexity.

# What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?

This project helped me develop spatial reasoning in 3D environments, especially understanding how objects relate within a shared coordinate system. I also improved at designing under constraints, such as using low-polygon primitives, which forced me to think more creatively about efficiency and representation.

# What design process did you follow for your project work?

I followed a layered process: first identifying objects and layout, then breaking them into primitives, applying transformations, and finally refining with textures and lighting. This step-by-step approach made the project manageable and ensured each stage worked before moving forward.

# How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?

This structured approach can scale to more complex projects by keeping systems modular and manageable. Designing with constraints and building incrementally is especially useful in game development, where performance and organization are critical.

# How do I approach developing programs?

I approach development iteratively, building small pieces, testing them, and refining them over time. This project reinforced the importance of not trying to complete everything at once, but instead focusing on steady, incremental progress.

# What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?

I focused on modularity by using reusable functions like SetTransformations to reduce repetition. I also relied on visual debugging, making small adjustments to position, scale, and lighting while observing the results in real time.

# How did iteration factor into your development?

Iteration was essential, as nearly every object and light required multiple adjustments to look correct. This process showed me that development is not linear and that refinement is a necessary part of achieving a polished result.

# How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones?

My approach evolved from trial-and-error into a more structured and intentional process. By the final project, I was organizing code into reusable systems and thinking about how components interact rather than just making individual features work.

# How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computer science gives me the foundation to understand and build complex systems rather than just using them. As a game designer, this allows me to solve problems more effectively and work more confidently with different tools and technologies.

# How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills for your educational pathway?

This course gave me a solid understanding of how 3D graphics systems work, including transformations, rendering, and lighting. These concepts provide a strong foundation for further study in graphics, simulation, and interactive systems.

# How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills for your professional pathway?

This project helped me understand how tools like Unity or Blender are built on the same core concepts I implemented manually. The biggest takeaway was seeing how those tools abstract complex systems into user-friendly workflows. As a game designer, this will help me use those tools more effectively and understand what is happening behind the scenes.
